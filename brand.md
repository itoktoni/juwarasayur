# Logo for "JUWARA SAYUR"

A clean and modern brand logo centered on a plain white background, featuring a combination of bold typography and organic graphic elements.

### Typography and Layout
* **Primary Text:** The word **"JUWARA"** is rendered in large, heavy, uppercase sans-serif lettering in a deep forest green. The letters are thick and closely set.
* **Secondary Text:** Directly below the first word, the word **"SAYUR"** is displayed in a slightly smaller, matching bold green font, also in all capital letters. The two words are vertically stacked and center-aligned.

### Graphic Elements
* **The Crown:** A small, five-pointed golden-yellow crown is positioned above the letters "JU" at the beginning of the top word. It has a simplified, flat design.
* **The Leaves:** Two stylized green leaves—one larger and one slightly smaller—are nestled in the center of the top word, placed between the "W" and the "R," adding a natural, organic touch to the brand identity.

### Color Palette
* **Forest Green:** The primary color used for all text and the leaf icons.
* **Golden Yellow:** Used exclusively for the crown icon.
* **White:** A neutral, bright background that makes the green and gold elements stand out.
